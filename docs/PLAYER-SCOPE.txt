X4 MULTIPLAYER - PLAYER SCOPE: NPC BEHAVIOUR KEYED TO "THE PLAYER"
25 October 2026 | INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT | Task D (Ferrite) | nothing below was run in X4

PROBLEM

Guests report that NPCs ignore them: targets at 0 m/s, no NPC fire, things
improve once the host arrives (reports/PLAYTEST-7.5.0-20261025.txt). On the host
the guest exists as an ordinary shared co-op ship: owner faction.player, created
by md/x4lan_bubble.xml PrepareShips without a pilot, moved by coop_game.lua
(NAV2 pose/velocity), named "LAN Player N". It is never player.occupiedship,
player.controlled or player.container on the host. Guest-side NPCs are inert
replicas (universe_game.lua, ActivateObject(false)); all NPC decisions are host
decisions. This document lists every vanilla path found that singles out the one
local player, classifies it, and records the first, inert-by-default slice.

Categories
 (a) works already for any faction.player ship (relation/ownership based)
 (b) keyed to player.* in MD/AI scripts: fixable with an extension diff patch or
     an additive MD cue (original code; stock files are never shipped)
 (c) engine-side: needs reverse engineering / a hook, or a UI relay to the guest

Evidence: vanilla md/ and aiscripts/ of 9.00 (base + ego_dlc_*), extracted
read-only to ../x4-re/gamescripts (outside the repo, not redistributed), and static
RE in ../x4-re/coord/reports/player.md. Line numbers refer to the base files.

SUMMARY COUNTS: 39 behaviours: (a) 8, (b) 22, (c) 9.

(c) Engine-side
 c1 Attention: Ship::ComputeAttention (vfunc703, rva 0x7d5110) gives ships in the
    player entity's parent chain level 9; NPCs near only the guest stay low/OOS,
    so <attention min="visible"> blocks (masstraffic.police, masstraffic.*
    handlers) never run and OOS combat is abstract. OWNED BY TASK A.
 c2 Mass traffic (including police mass traffic) is generated around the
    player's ship (U::MT functions near 0x8c3a90/0x8c4550 call the occupied-ship
    getter). No mass traffic near the guest unless engine-side.
 c3 player.occupiedship/controlled/container are derived from the single Player
    entity (*(Universe+0x230)); there is no list to add a second ship to. Script
    substitution (b1/b2/b4) is the only route without a hook.
 c4 Component::NotifyKiller (0x3e5490) also notifies the player entity only when
    the killer is the player-controlled ship (event_player_killed_object).
 c5 Gravidar registration (0x10b2820 -> GravidarUnit ctor 0x10ac0c0) flags the
    player-occupied ship; Gravidar::Run (0x10ae0e0) posts fog-of-war/discovery
    to the player entity; 0x10b1810 tracks the player's sector. Affects player
    knowledge/map, not NPC sensing (medium confidence).
 c6 Comm/hail/scan player actions (U::CommAction, U::BasicScanAction,
    CanPlayerCommTarget) require the local player; the guest's hails happen in
    the guest's own game against inert replicas. Needs request relay (R2).
 c7 IsInLiveView (Object vfunc799, 0x6d5900) depends on the player's controlled
    ship (radar live data); host-only knowledge.
 c8 Region damage fields (0xee1a50) take an extra branch for the occupied ship
    (likely feedback only; low confidence).
 c9 Missile lock/incoming warnings (Missile::Shoot 0x606f20, MissileLauncher
    vfunc988 0x609670) are player-ship specific. Cosmetic for the host.

No engine special case for the player ship was found in NPC combat target
selection or attack actions: none of the AI::/Scripts:: combat actions call the
player-ship getters or touch the Player singleton inline (search of 1,041 inline
users, medium confidence). Guest-visible "no NPC fire" is therefore attention
(c1) plus the guest's inert replicas not showing host NPC weapons, not a vanilla
refusal to target the guest.

FIRST SLICE (implemented, inert by default)
 Source config player_scope = false. Host only (slot p1), internal co-op only.
 - ui/player_scope_game.lua: after coop is ready, sends X4LAN_PlayerScope Setup,
   then the index of the shared ship that AUTH says p2 owns and pilots (0 = none)
   on change and every second. Queues halt notices (max 32) for R1; no network
   message is added.
 - md/x4lan_player_scope.xml: Session requires player.module x4lan_internal and
   Arena host+coop. Guest marks that ship with blackboard $x4lan_guestplayer
   (never the host's occupied ship or container) and clears the old mark. Halt
   answers 'halt' signals sent to the marked ship: police 'comply', pirates 'wait'
   (30 s), via 'player halt response' with a 1 s delay so the inspector is
   already waiting. Logs PLAYER_SCOPE_HALT and raises X4LAN_PlayerScope.Halt.
   No cargo, owner, relation, order or reward actions.
 - aiscripts/move.seekenemies.xml (diff): b1 at both sites, keyed only to the
   blackboard flag; non-blocking. Without the flag the stock expressions are
   unchanged in effect.
 Behaviour effect if enabled: pirates can pick the guest as a plunder target;
 police and pirates stop stalling 1-2 min at the guest; pirates grant 30 s and
 then attack unless wanted cargo was dropped (stock check). The guest is not told
 why (R1 pending). Combat engagement itself depends on c1 (Task A).
 Checks: tests/test_player_scope.py (inert defaults, adapter contract with
 engine doubles, MD/diff structure). dev/check_player_scope_diff.py applies the
 diff to a locally extracted stock move.seekenemies.xml (each selector must match
