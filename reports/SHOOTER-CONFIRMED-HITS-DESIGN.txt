SHOOTER-CONFIRMED HITS (SHOT): CURRENT DECISIONS AND BLOCKERS
Consolidated 24 Oct 2026 (Claude, after A:primary's native findings and reviews 02:11-02:39).
This replaces the earlier layered revisions. Status: transport and authority application are
implemented INERT (shot_claims=false, needs pilot_health). Capture and undo are NOT armed.

GOAL AND POLICY
Player-vs-player and player-vs-shared-target hits count as the shooter saw them. The FIRE2
replay keeps the native visuals and misses; intentional misses stay misses; no aim assistance.
NPC, collision and environment damage keep today's authority.

DECIDED (native evidence: A:primary last-attacker-01/02, NPC-03, damage-undo-02, channel-03)
D1 Identity: object.lastattacker inside each damage callback is the firing SHIP (34/34, also for
   8 different-attacker pairs at the same player.age). There is no verified weapon getter.
   lastattacktime is not refreshed on every hit and is not used.
D2 Application: the authority applies claimed hits with apply_attackstrength(object=victim,
   attacker=its copy of the shooter, hullshield=per-shot strength, hullonly=shieldonly=
   hullnoshield=0); all four channels must be given. Qualified in visible attention on the
   X Shuttle: exact ordinary shield-only, overflow and hull results; a final hit at the 1% floor
   leads to the existing LifeTick SHIP_LETHAL.
D3 Scope: only a uniform, unmodified loadout of ordinary hullshield-only stock bullets without
   turrets. The library entry (GetMacroData(macro,'infolibrary') + GetLibraryEntry) must give
   hullshielddpshot > 0 and every other per-shot/area channel 0, and GetInstalledWeaponMod must
   report no mod. The per-shot strength comes from that entry (X Shuttle plasma 40, pulse Mk1 36,
   Mk2 46). Shooter and authority must have equal digests (macro:strength). Anything else
   refuses claims.
D4 Transport: SHOT|1|lease|shooter|shooterGen|victim|victimGen|seq|hits|digest, cumulative
   hit counts. Generations are the relay's health generations; LAN targets use 0. The relay
   accepts it only from the owner+pilot of the shooter at its current generation, keeps seq and
   hits monotonic, and forwards it to the victim's authority (ship 2 -> its pilot while HEALTH
   is live, otherwise the host), never back to the shooter's own slot (there its hits are
   native). Ended generations are dropped.
D5 Atomicity: Lua issues Apply{base, count} (at most 20 hits) with the HEALTH readback window. MD
   compare-and-sets its accepted count, so the count and the native hits change together.
   Generations only move forward and a newer pair restarts from 0. Lua advances only on Applied
   and retries after 0.5 s. A failure stops the module permanently and zeroes MD strengths.
D6 Restore: synchronous restoration inside the callback works (68/68, original values visible to
   the canonical hit event, no recursion or death) and keeps callbacks alive at min 1/2.

REJECTED: time-bucket or same-frame correlators; lastattacktime freshness; zero-strength identity
reset (native: lastattacker stays); nominal-amount filtering as freshness; lower local floors;
observed shield/hull split replay; weapon attribution for mixed loadouts.

BLOCKERS BEFORE CAPTURE/UNDO MAY BE ARMED (A:primary native ownership)
B1 Stale identity: a collision, region or repair callback after a real hit may still read the
   old lastattacker. Collision freshness is unqualified; without it no callback may be claimed.
B2 Callback dedup: one hit gives a shield callback and, on overflow, a hull callback. Candidate
   to qualify (A:primary): count the shield callback; count a hull callback only when the
   restored current shield is zero (the shield undo runs synchronously before the matching hull
   callback, 34/34). Shield-piercing, area and beam weapons are out of scope.
B3 HEALTH proxy floor: the 2% proxy floor loses shooter callbacks once the canonical hull is
   <= 2% (the ship is alive until 1.01%). If SHOT uses a 1% floor, the host LifeTick must read
   the live remote authority's canonical health instead of the transient proxy hull, and
   release/watchdog must restore the right floor and value.
B4 Equal game data on both machines beyond the library digest (installed equipment mods are
   checked; macro library values are not proof against altered game files).

PRIMARY HARDENING AFTER cf58725 REVIEW (24 Oct, implementation still inert)
Review issuecomment-6377919689 reproduced four adapter/MD failures and a relay
authority-boundary error. Corrections on contrib/astra-shot-hardening:
- Native State admission/ACK binds Apply to current generations and victim
  authority; execution also checks HEALTH closed generations/live/own/auth and
  the State age. A permanent native Stop cannot be rearmed by delayed messages.
- Applied prefixes re-acknowledge without damage, recovering a lost Lua callback.
- Each pending count retains its admitted digest/strength. A changed admitted
  loadout stops SHOT for this load; it never reinterprets earlier counts.
- Only the three natively checked stock macros (XShuttle plasma40, pulse36/46)
  are admitted, with explicit non-beam and complete zero-other-channel data and
  no equipment mods. This is a bounded internal gate, not general weapon support.
- A shooter that is already victim authority never stores/sends claimed hits.
  The relay also refuses those records and requires a guest's acknowledged
  health baseline; native hits cannot later reappear as HACK-era claims.
- Native claimed damage is marked shotApplying for a future capture exclusion;
  dead/destroyed victims are not repeatedly attacked inside a batch.

NEW NATIVE EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
Primary damage-dedup-06 proves273 actual stock hits counted once across three
shield states and excludes a real own-source collision (see primary report
LAST-ATTACKER-NATIVE.txt). Same-pair collision+projectile in one frame remains
ambiguous. Use all collision pairs for that frame, never only the last collider.
HP undo alone is insufficient: recharge08 control starts recharging after9.0214s,
whereas17 undone real hits delay it to14.7183s. An original zero-damage dev bullet
leaves recharge at9.0386s, but physical impact/visual fidelity and safe weapon
substitution remain unqualified. Do not enable replay damage undo as a complete
solution. In-flight shots across handovers, native loadout queries, floor/life
coordination and whole capture-to-application protocol still need qualification.
