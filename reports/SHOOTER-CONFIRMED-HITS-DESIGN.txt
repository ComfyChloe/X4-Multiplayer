SHOOTER-CONFIRMED PER-HIT DAMAGE (design only; no code, no activation, no native run)
24 Oct 2026, Claude, at A:primary's request after last-attacker-01: inside every native damage
callback, target.lastattacker/lastattacktime matched the canonical per-projectile
player_ship_hit attacker/time, switching within ~0.07 s. Unlike the throttled attack
notifications (PR #34, withdrawn), this is a per-hit source primitive, still to be qualified
for same-frame overlap.

POLICY (one rule everywhere, so nothing is counted twice)
Player-vs-player and player-vs-shared-target hits count as the SHOOTER saw them, in the
shooter's world. Everything else (NPCs, collisions, environment) counts where the victim's
authority lives today. Concretely, for every victim V and every machine M:
 U  Undo: on V's authority machine, a native hit on V whose lastattacker is the REPLAYED remote
    player ship (the FIRE2 proxy) is given back immediately, inside the same MD damage
    callback (set_object_shield/hull += damage). That is the replay's copy of a shot whose
    real outcome the shooter reports.
 C  Claim: on the shooter's machine, a native hit by its OWN piloted co-op ship on a remote
    player's ship or a shared host-authoritative target is reported as cumulative damage.
 A  Apply: V's authority machine applies the increase of those totals natively.
Victim authority machine: ship 1 (host-piloted) -> host; ship 2 (guest-piloted, HEALTH live)
-> guest, whose HEALTH reports then carry the result to the host proxy; LAN targets / shared
NPC objects -> host.

NO DOUBLE COUNTING
- Every player-vs-player hit exists twice: native in the shooter's world (claimed, C) and as a
  FIRE2 replay in the victim's world (undone, U). Exactly one survives (the claim). A replay
  shot that misses in the victim's world has no copy to undo; a shooter's miss produces no
  claim. Intentional misses stay misses. There is no aim assistance.
- The host's OWN hits on the guest proxy in the host world are native, but HEALTH overwrites that
  proxy with the pilot's value, so they vanish; the host's claim (C) to the guest pilot is the
  one copy. On the guest, the host's replayed shots on the guest's own ship are undone (U).
  Symmetric.
- Attribution per callback: lastattacker at callback time. Ambiguity rule (bounded,
  conservative): if one game time sees damage callbacks on V with more than one distinct
  lastattacker, or a lastattacker that is null or destroyed, those callbacks are neither undone
  nor claimed. The native result stands. That is the residual error to measure.
- Child components: hull events only where damaged component == V (as PR #34). Shield events on
  V itself.

HP FLOOR AND DEATH
- U runs inside the damage callback, so it lands before any life tick (event cues run at
  callback time; LifeTick is a 0.05 s checkinterval cue). Player ships keep min hull 1%, so a
  replay hit can never destroy a ship before its undo.
- Death stays with the victim authority: a claim that brings V to <= 1.01% leads to the existing
  LifeTick (ships) or the LAN-target floor kill (targets keep the 1% floor while claims are
  active; a claim reaching the floor destroys, as in PR #34).
- Shields: an undo restores at most the damage of that callback, capped at maxshield. A claim
  applies shield then hull as reported (the shooter's callbacks already split them).

EPOCH, AUTHORITY AND TRANSPORT (reuses the HEALTH fences)
- SHOT|1|lease|shooterEntity|shooterGen|victim|victimGen|seq|shieldTotal|hullTotal: cumulative
  totals per (shooter lease, shooter entity, shooter generation, victim, victim generation).
  The relay accepts it only from the slot that owns AND pilots the shooter entity, in its
  current HEALTH-style generation. It forwards it only to the victim's current authority machine
  (ship 1 / targets -> host; ship 2 -> its pilot while its HEALTH generation is live; otherwise
  host). A victim generation change starts new totals, so nothing is applied twice across
  handovers.
- The victim applies only increases, in seq order, as Lua -> MD writes with generation fences
  and the same wall-second/game-age window as HEALTH. Refused writes are re-issued from the
  totals, which stay monotonic.
- Any AUTH transition of the shooter or victim ends the affected totals (as HEALTH generations).
- Pause, stall and failure: the same rules as HEALTH. No claim is applied without a fresh
  readback window. A failure closes the module permanently.

INTERACTION WITH HEALTH
- Required: the ship 2 victim path needs HEALTH live (the pilot applies claims, then reports).
  Without HEALTH, ship 2 stays host-authoritative and receives claims on the host proxy, with
  the guest's undo (U) off.
- HEALTH's 2% proxy floor and generation fences are unchanged. Claims are just another local
  damage source in the pilot's world.

NATIVE QUALIFICATION NEEDED (A:primary, before any activation)
1. Two attackers hitting V in the same game frame: does each callback see its own
   lastattacker, or the last one? This decides whether the ambiguity rule fires rarely or often.
2. Order within one hit: is lastattacker updated before BOTH shield_damaged and hull_damaged?
3. Are lastattacker/lastattacktime stale for callbacks with no attacker (collision, area,
   hull repair)? Does lastattacktime == player.age identify a fresh value?
4. Missiles and bombs: is lastattacker the launching ship?
5. set_object_hull/shield inside a damage callback: no recursion or extra events (your earlier
   capture says setters emit none).
6. Undo at the 1% floor: is the reported damage the applied amount when clamped at min hull?

REVISION 1 (A:primary review 02:15-02:19; supersedes the ambiguity rule, the timestamp idea and
the shield/hull application above). Still design only; no approval, no code.
1 Attribution: per callback, lastattacker inside that callback only (last-attacker-02: 34/34
  correct, including 8 different-attacker pairs at the same player.age). No time-bucket
  correlator, no same-frame rejection. lastattacktime is NOT used: it is not refreshed on every
  hit.
2 Stale identity (collision, environment or repair after a real hit keeps the old
  lastattacker): per-callback amount filter instead of a timestamp. A callback counts only when
  its damage matches a nominal per-hit damage of the claimed attacker's equipped bullets
  (macro <damage value> from the game's own bullet macros, e.g. plasma Mk1 720, read from the
  installed game on BOTH machines and compared at session start; a mismatch disables claims).
  The tolerance is a small epsilon, or "<= nominal" only when the callback is the floor-clipped
  one (new hull == floor). Collisions and region damage practically never equal a bullet value.
  The residual is measurable.
3 Shield/hull semantic: no ratios and no observed-split replay. The authority applies the
  claimed hit through the game's own damage primitive, apply_attackstrength (stock
  fight.attack.object.*.xml use it OOS), with attacker = its copy of the shooter. The channel
  comes from the bullet class: hullshield for normal bullets (shields first, overflow to hull
  at the authority's REAL shield state); hullonly / shieldonly / hullnoshield only where the
  bullet macro defines them. The claim carries (bullet macro id, hit count), not HP numbers. So
  differing shield states between the views are resolved natively at the authority, with the
  engine's kill credit and its own floor handling. Open: does apply_attackstrength in the
  attention (in-sector) zone apply the same per-bullet values as a real projectile? It must be
  qualified natively before anything else.
4 Floor clipping (proven blocker for observed-delta claims): with (2)+(3) the clipped amount is
  only a hit signal, not the damage applied. The remaining gap is "no hull callback at the
  floor": on the shooter side, the proxy of a remote player keeps a lower local min hull
  (0.1%) than the authority's death threshold (1.01%). WORLD keeps resetting it to the
  authority value, so the shooter's hits keep producing callbacks until the authority has
  declared death. The authority's own floor and life tick stay unchanged. Must be qualified
  natively: a fractional set_object_min_hull, and no local destruction at 0.1%.
5 Undo side (replayed remote shots on the victim): the same per-callback identity + amount
  filter. The undo restores exactly that callback's shield/hull inside the callback. At the
  victim's own floor the replay produces no callback, so there is nothing to undo.
NEW BLOCKERS before approval: (a) qualify apply_attackstrength in-sector (amount, channels,
attacker credit, floor, death); (b) qualify fractional local min hull on a remote proxy;
(c) equal bullet tables on both machines (mods); (d) mixed weapon groups where two equipped
bullets share a nominal value are ambiguous: those hits are dropped.
