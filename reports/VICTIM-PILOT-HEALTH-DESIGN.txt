VICTIM-PILOT HEALTH AUTHORITY (option A) - DESIGN AND SOURCE FEASIBILITY
23 Oct 2026, Claude (A:primary assignment 19:24 CEST). Base f29d3ef (7.3.0). DESIGN ONLY:
no production change, not release-ready, no native run. Damage stays as in 7.3.0.

WHY
PR34 (HIT2) is withdrawn. Native Linux evidence on c5eae79 (A:primary, 19:10/19:24) showed
that every attack notification is throttled to roughly one per second: event_object_attacked,
event_object_attacked_object and the player-owned variants alike. Damage events are per hit
and carry no source. A per-hit attacker exists only for the player's own controlled ship
(event_player_ship_hit). Option A needs no attacker at all: the pilot's machine is the
authority for the health of the co-op ship it pilots. All damage that the ship takes in the
pilot's own world counts: local NPCs and the FIRE2-replayed shots of the other player.

CURRENT SOURCE (f29d3ef)
- Host health: x4lan_bubble.xml Publish (0.2 s) sends hullpercentage/shieldpercentage of both
  co-op ships in WORLD. Guest Apply (bubble_game.lua) sets both ships' hull/shield from it on
  every change, and at least every 1 s.
- Death: x4lan_life.xml Tick (0.05 s), host only. A co-op ship with hull <= 1.01% (ships have
  min hull 1, x4lan_bubble.xml:16) or not operational becomes Arena.$dead. The occupant is
  ejected, then the ship is destroyed. The guest follows through WORLD (v <= 1.01 -> dead).
  The relay dead() reads the same WORLD values.
- Authority: x4lan/coop.py owners/epochs/pilot(entity). AUTH carries the revision, owner,
  epoch and pilot of each ship. A HELLO with a new lease increments the epoch of the ships
  that slot owns. coop_game.lua mirrors this as c.owners/c.epochs/c.pilots.
- Result today: the guest ship's health is decided in the host world, where the host sees the
  guest ship one latency late. Damage the guest takes from its own local NPCs is overwritten.

PROPOSED WIRE: HEALTH|1|lease|entity|epoch|seq|hull%|shield%   (pilot -> relay -> other peer)
- Sent by the machine whose slot both OWNS and PILOTS the entity (c.owners and c.pilots
  equal cfg.slot), at 10 Hz and at once on a decrease. The host never sends HEALTH for a ship
  it pilots: its native value is already the WORLD value.
- Relay (coop.py, like FIRE2): accept only when owner == pilot == sender slot, the epoch is
  current and the ship is not dead(). seq must be > the last seq of the same lease+epoch.
  Values must be finite, 0..100. Keep the latest frame and forward it to the other peer for
  0.75 s. A new epoch drops the old frame.
- Order: one monotonic seq per lease/entity/epoch. A stale or out-of-order frame is dropped.
  After an epoch change, nothing applies until the new pilot's first frame, which is the
  baseline. Until then the host keeps its native value.

HOST RULES (the remotely piloted proxy, entity e)
1. On each accepted HEALTH: set_object_shield and set_object_hull to the reported values.
   The host's own hits on the proxy are therefore overwritten, symmetric to the guest today.
2. While e is remotely piloted with a current epoch, the proxy gets set_object_min_hull 2. So
   host-world damage can never reach the 1.01% LifeTick threshold between frames. This
   removes the life race that killed PR34.
3. Lethal: a reported hull <= 1.01 restores min hull 1 and sets the reported value. LifeTick
   then declares death exactly as today (eject, destroy, WORLD, relay dead()).
4. Pilot loss or transfer (new epoch, pilot '0', HELLO lease change): restore min hull 1 and
   keep the current native value. The host is authority again until a new pilot's baseline.
   No health is invented.
5. The WORLD the host publishes then carries the pilot's value back to the pilot: an echo.

GUEST RULES (the ship it pilots, entity e)
6. WORLD Apply must not write hull/shield of e while the guest owns and pilots e in the
   current epoch. Otherwise the echo, about one RTT late, would heal or re-damage it.
   Source change: one condition in x4lan_bubble.xml Apply, keyed on a session value that
   coop_game.lua maintains from AUTH. This needs A:primary coordination (bubble owner).
7. Dead state still comes from WORLD (v <= 1.01). Locally the guest ship stays at the 1% min
   hull; the guest never destroys itself (unchanged: the coop LifeTick branch is host-only).

FILES (estimate)

LIMITS (state them in any release note)
- Victim-favoured: a shot counts when it hits in the victim's world. A shooter may see a hit
  that does not count, or the reverse, by about one latency of relative motion. That is the
  same lag as today, moved to the side whose health it is.
- Co-op ships only. LAN targets, Arena.$donor (the test freighter, min hull 100 by
  x4lan_trade.xml), stations and ordinary NPC combat are unchanged.
- Shield regeneration runs locally on the pilot side. Host-side regeneration of the proxy is
  overwritten at 10 Hz.
- Lethal latency: death is declared on the host up to one HEALTH interval plus one-way
  latency after the pilot's hull reaches the floor.

UNVERIFIED NATIVELY
(a) Repeated set_object_hull/shield at 10 Hz on a moving, FIRE2-driven proxy: side effects on
    shield recharge delay or effects. A:primary's capture shows setters emit no damage events.
(b) min hull 2 on the proxy holds against heavy bursts. Same mechanism as the existing 1%,
    but a different value.
(c) The guest Apply guard leaves no other writer of the piloted ship's health.

SMALLEST HONEST NEXT TEST (native, after authorization; no human playtest needed first)
One Linux host + synthetic or second native guest, profile-isolated, pilot_health=true:
1. The guest-piloted ship takes 10 replayed host shots in the guest world. Expect: HEALTH seq
   rises monotonically; the host proxy hull equals the reported value within 0.3 s;
   no SHIP_LETHAL.
2. Host-world damage to the proxy without any guest damage. Expect: overwritten at the next
   frame; hull never below 2%; no SHIP_LETHAL.
3. Drive the guest ship to the floor. Expect: one SHIP_LETHAL and SHIP_DESTROYED on the host,
   and the guest dead via WORLD.
4. Pilot transfer mid-fight. Expect: frames of the old epoch are dropped; min hull 1 is
   restored; no health jump without a new baseline.
Evidence: HEALTH send/accept/apply counts, LifeTick lines, readbacks at 5 Hz.

IMPLEMENTATION STATUS (draft, pilot_health=false by default; A:primary review corrections 22:14)
- Generation: the relay keeps one generation per ship for each (owner, epoch, pilot). Any change
  starts a new one, including the same owner leaving and re-entering the pilot seat, and drops
  the stored frame.
- Canonical baseline: for an unacknowledged generation the relay repeats
  HBASE|1|pilotLease|entity|gen|hull|shield to the pilot, taken from the host's latest WORLD. The
  pilot writes it on the ship it occupies (MD Base, then Based) and answers
  HACK|1|lease|entity|gen. HEALTH|1|lease|entity|gen|seq|hull|shield counts only for that
  acknowledged generation. No first frame is guessed.
- Host fencing: Lua accepts only the current or a newer generation, in seq order. Every queued
  MD Apply/Release carries its generation, and MD executes it only when that generation is
  >= Arena.$healthGen and the ship is operational and not dead.
- Freshness: no accepted frame for 1 s, or an owner/pilot/epoch change, releases the 2% floor
  (back to 1%, native value).
- Canonical WORLD: Publish sends Arena.$healthValue (the last applied pilot value) for a ship in
  Arena.$healthLive, not the proxy readback. So WORLD no longer depends on proxy damage or
  regeneration. LifeTick death still publishes 0.
- Guest guards in x4lan_bubble.xml: the ship setters are skipped and dead is assigned only on
  the host-confirmed 0 while Arena.$healthOwn.{i} is set (only after Based). Without the flag
  list (feature off) the expressions are unchanged.
- x4lan_bubble.xml was edited directly: dev/build_bubble_md.py no longer reproduces the
  committed file (its output differs in 45 lines on f29d3ef), so the generator was not run.
Tests: tests/test_health.py (relay baseline/ack, validity/order/staleness, leave/re-enter
generation; LuaJIT pilot baseline/ack/report, leave seat, host generation order/lethal/
staleness/transfer release, flag/ui/bubble guard text). 94 focused tests pass. Both MD files
validate against md.xsd.

Second review round (A:primary, re-review of c7cb744):
- Validity is bound to the AUTH revision. The relay sends HGEN|1|lease|authRevision|gen1|gen2 in
  every snapshot. Every owner/epoch/pilot change bumps that revision, so a generation is valid
  on a machine only once an HGEN of the AUTH revision it holds names it. HBASE, HEALTH and Based
  count only for that valid generation. A frame that overtook its AUTH is ignored; its sender
  repeats it.
- Every accepted AUTH transition of a ship closes every generation used so far, at once: the
  guest revokes live/basing, sets MD authority 0 and never re-adopts; the host releases the
  floor (closed). A newer valid generation also ends the older one (host: closed Release).
- The MD readback carries MD's game clock and the authority it was taken under. A reading taken
  before the baseline can never be the first frame of a generation.
- Queued Base/Apply carry a deadline on MD's game clock (TTL 1 s) that MD checks at execution.
  Without a readback clock nothing is queued, and nothing is queued while paused.
Third review round (A:primary on b88909f) and lifecycle self-audit:
- Real-time freshness (corrected after review): MD's own wall clock, as qualified in
  x4lan_attachment.xml. The readback (unpaused only) carries
  player.systemtime.{'%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S'} and player.age. Queued Base/Apply carry that second and
  the [age, age + 1 s] window. MD executes them only when player.timewarp.factor > 0s, in the
  same wall second and inside the window. Lua issues writes only from a readback at most 0.5 s
  old and not while paused, and simply re-issues refused ones. No Lua-written clock and no
  blackboard API.
- Base retry: an unconfirmed Base is re-issued after 0.5 s. MD writes a generation's baseline
  once ($healthBased) and only confirms repeats, so a retry never resets HP.
- Reports: each fresh MD readback is sent at most once. Nothing is sent while paused or from a
  reading older than 0.3 s; there is no cached republishing.
- Host close: every closed generation is sent to MD as a closed Release, even after a stale
  release removed the floor. A delayed queued Apply of it is refused natively.
- Watchdog: host MD returns a live ship to host authority, from the canonical value, after 2 s
  of game time without an applied pilot value. On a Lua failure the guest clears its MD
  authority (best effort).
Lifecycle audit (code and doubles only):
- Start: no generation is valid until an HGEN of the held AUTH revision; no HBASE exists before
  a host WORLD.
- Handover: valid HBASE -> Authority(gen, false) -> Base (fenced, written once) -> Based (valid
  only) -> Authority(gen, true) -> HACK on the next HBASE -> HEALTH accepted by the relay ->
  host Apply (fenced, deadline).
- Steady state: fresh readbacks, 10 Hz, at once on a decrease. Host freshness is 1 s real time;
  the MD watchdog covers 2 s game time.
- Leave, re-enter, transfer, epoch change: the AUTH hook closes at once on both sides. The relay
  generation moves on; delayed frames are invalid.
- Pause: no new writes; queued writes expire by real time; reports stop; freshness releases.
- Death: a reported <= 1.01 restores the 1% floor -> LifeTick -> Publish 0 -> later writes are
  refused (not operational / dead).
- Accepted residual: a Base queued just before a revocation can still execute before the
  revoking Authority event. It writes the host's canonical baseline of the ended generation on
  the ship and its Based is then refused. No pilot value is accepted from it.
Still unverified natively: 10 Hz setters on a FIRE2-driven proxy, min hull 2 under bursts.
Stale, pause and failed-callback audit (after the MD wall-clock change):
- Pause: readbacks stop (timewarp gate), so Lua stops issuing writes within 0.5 s and stops
  reporting. Queued writes fail the timewarp/second/window check on resume. Host freshness
  (1 s real) and the watchdog (2 s game) release the floor. After resume, the next readback
  re-enables writes.
- Lua stalls or fails while MD runs: no new writes or reports. The host watchdog releases, and
  the guest clears its MD authority on a caught failure. A guest Lua that hangs without failing
  keeps $healthOwn: it remains authoritative for its own ship and the host releases its floor.
- MD refuses a write (second boundary, window, fence): Base -> no Based -> retried after 0.5 s
  from a fresh readback, written once. Apply -> the next HEALTH frame (10 Hz) re-issues it.
  Release is not windowed (restorative, fenced by generation).
- Lost or late network frames: the relay repeats HBASE/HGEN every snapshot. HEALTH counts only
  for the valid generation, in seq order. HACK is resent on each repeated HBASE.
