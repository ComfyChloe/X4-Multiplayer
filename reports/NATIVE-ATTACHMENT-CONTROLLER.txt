ship registry, plus their exact saved storage/external bay indices. The diagnostic
suspends the carrier and its docked children; it does not create a replacement pair
or load a normal save. Normal-save manifests and the actual owned installation/
profile backups are retained per run.

The Lua probe drives the real pure controller with synthetic fresh host intentions
for eight states: FREE, STORED, FREE, EXTERNAL, STORED, EXTERNAL, FREE, STORED.
A successful cycle requires nine native actions, including the initial store. It independently reads native attachment state,
uses fresh correlated saved-dock queries before attachments, retains native action
fences, and confirms each phase through a separate MD readback. Carrier position
is held in its existing undocked parent frame; no direct docked-child pose writes
are made. The selected carrier/child and local player ancestors are checked before
writes. Native MD repeats saved indices/references, identity, player ancestry,
expected previous attachment, target type/capacity/relative witness, compatibility
and occupancy checks at operation consumption. Tokens are consumed before mutation.

validate_attachment_controller.py independently requires selection agreement,
correct causal Lua dispatch/native action/Lua read/native read/complete order,
exact selected ship code and saved dock IDs, all expected transitions and fresh
correlated target readbacks. Missing/reordered actions, unrelated identities and
callbacks before query completion are rejected. This is functional engine evidence,
not an authenticated two-PC attachment test or complete-universe acceptance.
