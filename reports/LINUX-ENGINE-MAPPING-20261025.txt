L1: BOUNDED LINUX ENGINE ADDRESS RESEARCH
25 October 2026 | Astra | partial delivery for Ferrite

SCOPE
Static, read-only inspection of the user's installed Linux executable. No engine
patches, injection, native calls or modified binaries. The associated JSON holds
addresses, small identification signatures and evidence descriptions, not engine
source or decompiled code. Ferrite owns hook design, ABI review and activation.
H/M/L is confidence in STATIC identification, never native acceptance. Even H
entries require an exact-build check and appropriate ABI/thread/lifetime review.
