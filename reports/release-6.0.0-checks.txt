X4 LAN Lab 6.0.0 — verification and limits
10 October 2026 | X4 9.00 (611726), Steam build 23660954

Scope of evidence
-----------------
Native Linux X4 was tested as host and as guest, in separate fresh creative
worlds. Each counterpart was a deterministic local UDP peer. These tests run
real X4 MD/Lua/AI code, but are NOT a new Windows/Linux two-computer playtest.
The user's earlier real 5.1.0 session confirmed visibility, suit ejection and
station respawn. Windows 6.0.0, ZeroTier latency and long sessions need retesting.

Native host checks
------------------
PASS: an absolute position 200 km from spawn and another sector were transmitted.
Both local shared objects reached cluster_01_sector001_macro in the travel check.
Ships were retained, and original ship 1 transferred to player 2 while player 1
subsequently controlled original ship 2. Authority epochs rejected old owners.

PASS: 20 native shots from the synthetic guest's replicated ship affected the
host target, reducing its shield to 88.7304 percent.
PASS: the replicated ship left its dock and reached the other station, including
an intentionally missing approach update. Native docking used a local free pad.
PASS: actual native purchases and sales from BOTH transferred ships:
  Buy 10 Energy Cells at Alpha: 200,000 -> 199,860 credits.
  Sell those 10 at Beta from the remotely controlled ship: 200,030 credits.
  Buy another 10 using the other shared ship: 199,890 credits.
  Sell those 10 at Beta: 200,060 credits and combined mission delivery 20/20.
The common account paid for all four transactions. Proxy ship money stayed zero.

PASS: both targets were destroyed with deterministic engine damage in the
mission-completion check. The mission recorded two kills and paid 25,000 credits
EXACTLY ONCE: final shared balance 225,060 credits. Replayed delivery
completion did not add another reward. The earlier live shot check was separate;
this is not a claim that both entire kills were manually flown over a network.
PASS: destruction while controlling the other original ship caused ejection;
a suit hit led to station respawn without Game Over.

Native guest checks
-------------------
PASS: an arriving dock snapshot waited for local station setup; no missing-market
MD error remained. Local simulated lethal damage to a shared ship and target
was reconciled while the host still considered both alive.
PASS: host-confirmed ship destruction caused ejection, then suit death caused
respawn at Alpha. The guest took the other shared ship's cockpit. The normal
native UndockPlayerShip(false) command returned granted, and both MD and the
native UI reported that it was no longer docked.
PASS: the controlled ship reached cluster_01_sector001_macro at (250000,10000,0)
metres, beyond the former movement boundary.
PASS: delivery/combat/completion mission states were mirrored. Completed and
older snapshots were replayed without regression or a guest-side reward call.
Both host-confirmed target deaths removed their guest replicas.
PASS: all guest network packets were dropped for 40 seconds. After the 30-second
timeout the game resumed the SAME lease, retained ship authority/epoch and the
completed mission, and displayed 225,000 credits from the synthetic host's
200,000 + 25,000 balance. This scenario intentionally performed no actual trades.
The guest ended with Game Over false. No X4LAN MD/Lua runtime error remained.

Performance evidence
--------------------
Version 6 real UDP loopback relay benchmark: 20.012 seconds, two synthetic
peers, navigation/crew/body/world/economy/quest/metrics traffic and status output.
CPU: 4.27% of ONE logical core. Peak RSS: 17.6 MiB.
Receive/transmit: 138.6/197.5 packets/s, 0 send errors.
99th-percentile relay step: 0.976 ms.
This excludes X4, ZeroTier and the terminal emulator. Moving the small relay alone
is therefore not established as a solution to poor laptop game performance.

A separate native creative-world baseline disabled LAN gameplay, networking and
fixture creation, while retaining the same 1080p exclusive Low profile and the
same disabled third-party selection. After 30 seconds of settling, it collected
47 callback windows: median 15.79 callbacks/s, median
window maximum gap 71.16 ms. Active multiplayer scenes varied
substantially with location and being in a cockpit, suit or station room.
These are UI callback timings, NOT rendered FPS. Different generated worlds and
scenes prevent a controlled percentage attribution to the mod or GPU driver.
Unchanged pose/cargo writes were reduced, with periodic authoritative refresh.
No general FPS improvement or resolution of the laptop bottleneck is claimed.

Remaining scope limits
----------------------
Shared: the two original ships, two targets, two assigned cargo containers,
Alpha/Beta's six supported wares, common treasury, and Cooperative Trial.
The normal trade interface works at Alpha/Beta for the ship currently piloted.
General NPC markets, universe economy, construction, arbitrary cargo magnets,
vanilla plots/missions and session save/load are not synchronized.
Sector-aware movement is native-tested; actual two-game gate/highway/travel-drive
and passenger behaviour still require a playtest. Walking avatars remain off.
Friendly fire stays enabled. Suit weapons are not replicated. Respawn searches
accessible non-hostile stations in the current sector; it waits if none exists.
Destroyed ships/cargo are not replaced. An uncertain native trade needs fresh
worlds, not a second request. Restarting either game or relay requires both new
worlds. Both endpoints must use the complete matching 6.0.0 pair.
