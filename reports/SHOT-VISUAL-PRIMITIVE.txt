LIMITS / NEXT IMPLEMENTATION
No source capture, network role transition, near-overheat/burst-state continuity,
free-aim replay or Windows acceptance follows these isolated primitives. Native
shoot_at is a diagnostic control only; production must not auto-lock a selected
victim or remove native scatter/misses. Source installed gun macros stay stock
even when their launched bullet is zero: capture requires a separate verified
original-bullet mode, not merely the stock loadout/mod gate.

Primary owns an INERT visual-mode module and FIRE2 fences. Proposed native proof:
shotVisualMode (0unknown/1original/2zero), shotVisualGen, shotVisualAt, plus ordered
request/proof serials. Stop native scripted fire and clear finite FIRE2 budgets,
wait for matching native ACK, then switch/read back all actual installed guns.
Only then publish mode proof under the current SHOT generation/state/time window.
Lua and native FIRE2 execution must both require current zero-mode proof; queued
old firing requests must never fire a newly restored original weapon. Capture
requires fresh matching original-mode proof. All flags remain off pending review
and focused native integration. In-flight damage across authority handovers is
an additional qualification, not solved merely by changing future bullets.
